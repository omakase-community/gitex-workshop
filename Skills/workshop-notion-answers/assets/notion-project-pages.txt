PAGE-N04 | Request Handoff - Working Design Notes
Owner: Alex Morgan
Last edited: September 4, 2026, 11:40 Eastern
Status: Internal working draft; not delivered to Harborline

Working problem: a customer request crosses systems and teams, but the answer does not reliably return to the person responsible for responding. The draft should make the handoff visible without adding another daily portal for account managers. Lena said their normal starting places are HubSpot and their inboxes.

Candidate record fields: request summary, source reference, relationship owner, current action owner, last operational answer, next action, and commitment date with its basis. A relationship owner can remain unchanged while the action owner moves from dispatch back to Sales. Reassignment should retain history rather than overwrite the previous owner's name.

A date might be an explicit promise, an internal target, or a suggestion. The design needs to preserve that distinction. “Urgent” alone is not a dated commitment. A source reference is useful when someone needs to check why an item is shown as due.

Possible first view: one account manager sees requests awaiting an operational answer; an operations lead sees the answers they own; a manager sees exceptions. These are design ideas, not screens agreed by Harborline. No implementation tool has been chosen.

For customer-facing messages, the proposed flow ends at a draft awaiting human review. Lena was not ready for automatic replies from transcripts. Kivo can explore internal suggestions without assuming permission to send.

The map will separate today's workflow, possible changes, and open questions. This working page is not the promised one-page deliverable, and no delivery to the customer has been recorded.
