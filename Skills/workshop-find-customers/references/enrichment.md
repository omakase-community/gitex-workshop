# Optional people research and enrichment

Load this after the participant asks about contacts, decision-makers, Apollo, or LinkedIn. The company shortlist is already a valid result; enrichment is an extension, not an unfinished prerequisite.

## Clarify the missing information

Ask which roles or contact details would help the next action. Target one or two relevant decision-makers per chosen company instead of exporting all staff. Reuse the agreed ICP and company identifiers.

Offer the applicable route without making the participant install a tool:

- **Public research:** Look for relevant leaders on company websites, team pages, speaker biographies, and attributable announcements. Show a name and role only with supporting evidence; mark missing or uncertain information explicitly. A likely role is useful even when a current name cannot be verified.
- **Connected enrichment:** A service such as Apollo may add person and company information, subject to account access, available capabilities, and credits. Read the shared [connection guidance](../../workshop-decide/references/connections.md) before setup and the [Apollo requirements](../../workshop-decide/references/provider-routes.md#fireflies-and-apollo) before using Apollo, including an existing connection. Discover the actual tools and documented requirements; obtain consent for any credit-consuming operation, then run a small, agreed batch. A service returning an email address is not evidence that it is current or deliverable unless the returned status supports that statement.
- **Manual inspection:** The participant may inspect LinkedIn themselves and provide relevant notes or a URL to discuss. Keep browser automation away from harvesting LinkedIn profiles or employee lists.

Apollo's official [People Enrichment documentation](https://docs.apollo.io/docs/enrich-people-data) explains the provider workflow. Verify current access and charging requirements before promising a live operation. Do not guess an MCP endpoint, connection command, contact email, or email pattern when the necessary capability or evidence is missing.

## LinkedIn boundary

LinkedIn's [prohibited software and extensions policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387/prohibited-software-and-extensions?lang=en) restricts automated access and scraping. An authenticated browser session is not authorization to scrape profiles. Automated staff extraction, bulk profile collection, CAPTCHA bypass, and automated LinkedIn outreach are outside this workshop route. Use permissible public company sources or the participant's own manual inspection instead.

## Make the result auditable

Preserve the company match, person's role, source or provider, and any verification status. Keep missing values visibly missing. For fictional offline companies, stay within the fictional exercise or ask to switch to real companies; do not submit their invented names or `.example` domains to live enrichment and present matches as the same businesses.

Stop when the requested contacts have evidence or the access/data limit is clear. Offer the company-only result without framing it as a failure.
