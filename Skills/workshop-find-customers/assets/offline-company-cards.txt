FICTIONAL OFFLINE COMPANY CARDS — CODEX SALES WORKSHOP

These five companies, people, facts, quotations, and source IDs are invented exercise data. They are not search results or verified leads. Domains ending in .example are placeholders, not live websites. Source IDs identify supplied fictional snippets only; never cite them as real web evidence or submit them to a live enrichment service.

Use only when the participant chooses the offline example. The goal is to practice explaining fit, distinguishing evidence from inference, and refining a customer segment. A company card is not proof that a company wants to buy. Some records deliberately contain gaps. The five cards are candidates to qualify, not five pre-approved ideal customers.

Source type: Fictional company announcement
Snippet: "Our new Midwest commercial team will work alongside operations to support a growing book of regional accounts. We are reviewing the handoff from a sales commitment to the operating team that delivers it."

Source ID: OFFLINE-HL-JOB
Source type: Fictional operations systems job posting
Snippet: "The systems coordinator will reconcile CRM account details with transport-management records and help sales and operations maintain consistent ownership of customer follow-ups."
